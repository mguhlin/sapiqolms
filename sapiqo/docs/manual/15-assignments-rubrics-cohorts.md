# Assignments, rubrics and cohorts

Use **Courses → More actions → Assignments and rubrics** to create submission
assignments. Choose a reusable rubric or a single points score. Rubric criteria
use `Criterion | maximum points`, one per line; their total determines the
assignment maximum. Each assignment copies its rubric so later rubric creation
does not silently alter existing scores.

Choose a passing percentage, optional release/deadline with an explicit timezone
(e.g. `2026-10-15T17:00:00-05:00`), late policy and resubmission option. Mark
required assignments before enrolling a cohort. Existing gradebook assessments
continue working as scores-only assessments.

Only administrators and **explicitly assigned course instructors** may review
learner work. Content-author permission alone does not grant student-data access.
Administrators assign/remove instructors by an existing account's email from the
course assignment page. Assigned instructors get a **Your teaching courses**
section on their dashboard, can review submissions and enter rubric scores or
points plus feedback. These scores appear in the administrator gradebook.

Cohort schedules apply to groups in the course. Non-admin instructors see groups
containing enrolled learners. Schedules control course release and override
assignment deadlines. Overlapping group memberships use the latest release,
earliest deadline and strictest late policy. Store dates in UTC and explain the
schedule to learners. Blank dates remove a schedule.

Stale draft/grade saves are rejected; reload rather than overwriting newer work.
Accepted late submissions are identified in review. Required passing submissions
feed course completion and certificates. Administrators can use **Learners
needing attention** to review not-started, stalled, overdue or revision-needed
learners and follow links to enrollment and work.
