# Notifications

**Audience:** learner
**Where:** Notifications (the bell icon in the top navigation, or /notifications)

## What it is
Notifications keep you posted on things that happen in your account — being
enrolled in a course, earning a badge, and reminders that a course's access is
about to end. In-app notifications always work. If your organization has set up
email, some of these events also arrive in your inbox.

## How to use it
1. Look at the **bell icon** in the top navigation. A small badge on it shows how
   many unread notifications you have (it shows "9+" when there are more than
   nine).
2. Choose the bell (or open /notifications) to see your recent notifications,
   newest first.
3. Select any notification that links somewhere — for example a course or your
   transcript — to go straight there.
4. Opening the Notifications page marks everything as read, so the unread badge
   clears.

## Options & behavior
- **Unread badge** — the count of unread items shown on the bell; it disappears
  once you view the Notifications page.
- **Notification list** — your most recent items (unread ones are marked with a
  filled dot, read ones with a hollow dot), each with the time it was created.
- **Links** — many notifications link to the relevant place (a course to resume,
  your dashboard for a new badge, or your transcript when access ends).
- **Email (optional)** — when your administrator has configured email, certain
  notifications are also emailed. If email is not configured, everything still
  works in-app.

### What triggers a notification
- **Enrolled in a course** — "You are enrolled in ..." with a link to open it.
- **Badge earned** — "You earned the badge for ..." when you complete a course
  (and a congratulations email, if email is on).
- **Access ending soon** — reminders before a course's enrollment expires, sent at
  set milestones (by default about a month out, the week of, and the day before).
  Each reminder tells you the exact end date and how soon it is.
- **Access ended** — a notice when a course's access period has ended, reminding
  you that your badge, certificate, and CPE hours remain on your transcript.

## How it works
In-app notifications are stored on your account, so they show on any device and
never depend on email. Visiting the Notifications page marks all of your unread
items as read at once.

Expiry reminders are graduated: each milestone is sent at most once, and if the
system checks in late and you've crossed more than one milestone, you get a
single reminder for the most urgent one rather than a burst of old ones. Whether
these reminders also reach your inbox depends on whether your administrator has
enabled email — the in-app notification is sent either way. When a course's access
actually ends, the enrollment is soft-ended, meaning anything you already earned
stays on your transcript.

## Tips & gotchas
- The unread badge clears as soon as you open the Notifications page, even if you
  don't click any individual item.
- If you're not receiving emails, only in-app notifications are configured — check
  the bell.
- Expiry reminders are your cue to finish a time-limited course before access
  ends so you earn the badge and CPE hours in time.
- Nothing you've earned is lost when access ends — see your
  [Transcript](05-badges-certificates-transcript.md).
