# Assignments and feedback

Open **Assignments and feedback** from your dashboard course, or from the
course home. Each assignment shows its instructions, required/optional status,
maximum points, passing threshold, rubric and deadline (UTC).

1. Enter your response and optionally attach a PDF, text file, PNG or JPEG
   (maximum 10 MB). Save a draft to return later.
2. Submit for instructor review. Your instructor sees submitted work, not a
   separate submission notification by email.
3. Read feedback and rubric scores on the same page. If resubmission is allowed,
   revise and submit again. Saving a graded response clears its current score
   for another review.
4. Required assignments need a passing instructor grade in addition to lesson
   and quiz progress before a new course certificate is issued. Existing
   credentials remain on your transcript.

Course/cohort release dates and late policies are enforced on the server.
Group deadlines override assignment defaults. Attachments are private to you,
administrators and instructors explicitly assigned to your course.
