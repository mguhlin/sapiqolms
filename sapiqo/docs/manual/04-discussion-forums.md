# Discussion forums

**Audience:** learner
**Where:** Forum (top navigation → Forum, or /forum); course boards at
/forum/course/<slug>

## What it is
The Forum is where you read announcements and take part in course discussions.
The Forum hub has two parts: a global **Announcements** board that everyone with
an account can read (only administrators post there), and **Course discussions** —
per-module discussion boards inside the courses you're enrolled in. Course
authors decide which modules have a discussion board.

Discussions are threaded: you can start a new topic (a thread) or reply to any
existing post, and replies can nest under one another. You can also "like" posts.

## How to use it
1. Open **Forum** in the top navigation.
2. Read any **📣 Announcements** at the top of the page.
3. Under **💬 Course discussions**, choose a course to see its discussion boards
   grouped by module.
4. Choose a board to open it. Each board may show a discussion prompt from the
   author, then the posts.
5. To start a thread, write in the **Start a discussion** box and choose **Post**.
6. To reply, choose **↩ Reply** under a post, type your reply, and choose
   **Reply**. Your reply nests under that post.
7. To like or unlike a post, choose the **👍** button. The count updates
   immediately.
8. To remove your own post, choose **Delete** on it.

## Options & behavior
- **Announcements** — visible to everyone signed in; read-only for learners. Only
  administrators can post them, and administrators may pin an announcement to the
  top.
- **Course boards** — appear only for courses you are enrolled in (staff see all).
  You must be enrolled to read and post in a course's discussion.
- **Start a discussion** — creates a new top-level thread in the board.
- **↩ Reply** — creates a reply. A reply can target any post in the same board,
  and replies nest to show the conversation structure.
- **👍 Like** — toggles your like on a post; the visible count reflects everyone's
  likes.
- **Delete** — you can delete your own posts; deleting a thread also removes its
  replies. Moderators (administrators and group managers) can delete or hide any
  post.
- **"Post before you see others" gate** — some boards are set so you must
  contribute first. Until you post your own thread or reply in that board, you
  only see your own posts, with a note: "This forum is set to post before you see
  — share your own thoughts to reveal what others have posted."

## How it works
Boards are defined by the course's structure: a module's board exists only if the
author added a forum to that module in the editor — there are no automatic
per-module boards. Each course board is keyed to its course and module, and its
posts are stored as threads (top-level posts) and replies (posts with a parent),
building the nested tree you see.

The "post before you see others" setting is enforced when the board loads: if the
board is gated and you haven't posted yet, the page filters the thread list down
to only your own posts. As soon as you post, the full discussion is revealed.
Likes are stored per user per post, so each person's like counts once and the
button reflects whether you've already liked it.

Access is checked on every action: reading and posting in a course board require
an active enrollment in that course, and posting an announcement requires
administrator rights.

## Tips & gotchas
- If a course has no boards, no forums were added to its modules — that's up to
  the author.
- You won't see a course's discussion until you're enrolled in it.
- On a gated ("post before you see") board, posting anything — a thread or a
  reply — unlocks everyone else's posts for you.
- Deleting your own top-level post also deletes the replies underneath it.
- Announcements are one-way: read them here, but you can't reply to them.
