# Badges, certificates, and your transcript

**Audience:** learner
**Where:** Dashboard (Your badges) and Transcript (top navigation → Transcript,
or /transcript)

## What it is
When you complete a course, Sapiqo issues you a **badge** — an image credential
with a unique code. From that badge you can download a printable **certificate**
(a PDF), which includes any **CPE hours** the course awards. Every credential you
earn is also listed on your permanent **Transcript**, a printable record of your
completed courses and total CPE hours.

Your transcript is permanent: badges, certificates, and CPE hours stay there even
after a course's access period ends or the course itself is retired.

## How to use it
1. Complete a course (reach 100% of its steps). Your badge is issued
   automatically, and a "🎉 Course complete!" message offers an immediate badge
   download.
2. Open your **Dashboard** and find **Your badges**. Each badge card shows the
   course, the date, and the credential **Code**.
3. Choose **Badge (PNG)** to download the badge image.
4. Choose **Certificate (PDF)** to download the printable certificate.
5. Open **Transcript** (from the top navigation, or the **View transcript →**
   link on the dashboard) to see all your completed courses, CPE hours per course,
   and your total CPE hours.
6. On the transcript, choose **Print / Save as PDF** to keep or submit a copy;
   you can also download each badge or certificate from the **Credential** column.

## Options & behavior
- **Badge (PNG)** — downloads the badge image for that course.
- **Certificate (PDF)** — downloads a two-part PDF: page one is the text
  certificate (your name, the course, the date, CPE hours, and the provider
  line), and, if the course has badge art, page two is the earned badge image.
- **CPE hours** — the professional-development hours the course awards. They show
  per course and as a running total on your transcript. Courses with no CPE hours
  show a dash.
- **Credential code** — a unique code printed on each certificate so a credential
  can be verified.
- **Print / Save as PDF** (transcript) — prints a clean copy of your transcript,
  hiding navigation and buttons.
- **View transcript →** — the shortcut from your dashboard's badge section.

## How it works
Completion is measured server-side: when your completed steps reach 100% of a
course's total steps, Sapiqo marks the enrollment complete and issues the badge
(once — re-completing never creates a duplicate). At that moment it captures a
durable snapshot of the credential (title, course, date, and CPE hours) so your
record stays intact even if the course is later changed or removed.

The certificate PDF is generated on demand from that record and the course's badge
art — no external tools are needed. Only you (and administrators) can view or
download your own badge and certificate.

Your transcript reads from those stored credentials, falling back to the live
course details where needed, and it lists credentials even for courses that were
later removed. When an enrollment's access period expires, Sapiqo soft-ends it —
your progress, badge, certificate, and CPE hours all remain on your transcript
permanently.

## Tips & gotchas
- Your badge appears on the dashboard the instant you hit 100% — no waiting.
- The certificate is a PDF built for printing or submitting for CPE credit;
  download it whenever you need a copy.
- If email is configured, you also get a congratulations email when you complete a
  course.
- Even if a course becomes **Unavailable**, your earned badge and certificate stay
  on your dashboard and transcript.
- Use the credential **code** if someone needs to verify your certificate.
